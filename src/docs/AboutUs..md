# About Us: 

**H2 Lanka (Pvt) Ltd.** is a Sri Lankan clean energy and hydrogen technology company dedicated to accelerating the transition towards a **low-carbon, sustainable, and energy-secure future**. We focus on developing, integrating, and promoting innovative **green hydrogen and renewable energy solutions** for Sri Lanka and the wider South Asian region.

Our vision is to establish hydrogen as an important component of Sri Lanka’s future clean energy economy by connecting **renewable energy, green hydrogen production, energy storage, transportation, industrial applications, and emerging Power-to-X technologies**.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to provide practical, commercially viable, and technically sound clean hydrogen solutions that support industries in reducing fossil-fuel dependence and greenhouse-gas emissions.

We work to bridge the gap between **research, technology, policy, investment, and commercial implementation**, enabling promising hydrogen technologies to move from concept and demonstration towards real-world deployment.

### What We Do

H2 Lanka provides expertise and solutions across the emerging hydrogen value chain, including:

* **Green Hydrogen Production** – Renewable-energy-based hydrogen production using electrolysis.

* **Renewable Energy Integration** – Integration of solar, wind, and other renewable energy resources with hydrogen systems.

* **Hydrogen Storage and Distribution** – Technologies and solutions for safe and efficient hydrogen handling.

* **Hydrogen Mobility** – Hydrogen fuel-cell vehicles, refuelling infrastructure, and clean transportation solutions.

* **Industrial Hydrogen Applications** – Supporting industries in adopting hydrogen as a clean energy carrier and fuel.

* **Power-to-X Solutions** – Exploring hydrogen-based fuels, chemicals, ammonia, methanol, and other value-added products.

* **Pilot and Demonstration Projects** – Supporting the development of technology demonstration projects to establish technical and commercial feasibility.

* **Technical Consultancy and Project Development** – Providing engineering, feasibility, techno-economic, resource assessment, and project-development support.

* **International Technology Collaboration** – Connecting Sri Lankan organizations with leading international hydrogen technology providers, research institutions, investors, and industry partners.

### Innovation Through Research and Technology

H2 Lanka places strong emphasis on **research, innovation, and evidence-based technology development**. Our approach combines renewable-energy engineering, hydrogen technology, techno-economic analysis, energy-system modelling, and energy-policy perspectives.

We aim to identify solutions that are not only technically feasible but also **economically competitive, scalable, environmentally responsible, and appropriate for Sri Lanka’s energy system**.

### Building International Partnerships

The development of a hydrogen economy requires strong collaboration between governments, technology providers, researchers, investors, utilities, industries, and international organizations.

H2 Lanka therefore works to establish strategic partnerships with **Korean and international hydrogen technology companies, research institutions, renewable energy developers, financial institutions, and industrial stakeholders**.

Through these partnerships, we seek to facilitate **technology transfer, joint research, pilot projects, investment opportunities, capacity building, and commercialization** of hydrogen technologies in Sri Lanka.

### Our Focus on Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka has significant potential for renewable energy development, particularly in **solar and wind power**. These resources provide an important foundation for producing green hydrogen and developing new clean-energy applications.

H2 Lanka aims to contribute to this opportunity by supporting the development of a **Sri Lankan green hydrogen ecosystem** that can create new industries, strengthen energy security, reduce fossil-fuel imports, support industrial decarbonization, and create new investment and employment opportunities.

We believe Sri Lanka can develop hydrogen applications that serve both the **domestic market and future regional and international clean-energy markets**.

### From Ideas to Implementation

Our philosophy is simple:

``` Research → Innovation → Demonstration → Investment → Commercialization ```

We work with partners to transform promising hydrogen concepts into practical projects. From initial feasibility studies and technology selection to pilot-scale demonstration, investment facilitation, and commercial deployment, H2 Lanka seeks to provide an integrated pathway for hydrogen project development.
