# Our Focus

At **H2 Lanka (Pvt) Ltd.**, our focus is on accelerating the development and deployment of **green hydrogen and renewable energy technologies** that can deliver practical, scalable, and economically viable solutions for Sri Lanka and international markets.

We concentrate on the following key areas:

## Green Hydrogen Production

Developing renewable-energy-based hydrogen production systems using **solar, wind, and other renewable energy resources**, with a focus on improving efficiency, reducing production costs, and supporting large-scale deployment.

## Renewable Energy & Hydrogen Integration

Integrating renewable energy generation with **electrolyzers, energy storage, hydrogen storage, and smart energy management systems** to create reliable and flexible clean-energy solutions.

## Industrial Decarbonization

Supporting industries in reducing their dependence on fossil fuels through the application of **green hydrogen, hydrogen-derived fuels, and clean energy technologies** in industrial processes and energy-intensive applications.

## Hydrogen Mobility

Promoting hydrogen-based transportation solutions, including **fuel-cell electric vehicles, hydrogen refuelling infrastructure, and clean mobility systems**, particularly for heavy-duty and long-distance transportation.

## Power-to-X Technologies

Exploring opportunities to convert renewable electricity and green hydrogen into valuable products such as **green ammonia, green methanol, synthetic fuels, and other hydrogen-derived products**, creating new opportunities for Sri Lanka's export-oriented clean-energy economy.

## Pilot & Demonstration Projects

Facilitating **pilot-scale and demonstration projects** that validate emerging hydrogen technologies under Sri Lankan operating conditions and establish their technical, economic, and commercial feasibility.

## Technology Transfer & International Collaboration

Building strategic partnerships with **international technology providers, research institutions, investors, government agencies, and industry partners**, with particular emphasis on technology transfer, joint development, capacity building, and commercialization.

## Research & Techno-Economic Analysis

Applying **energy-system modelling, resource assessment, techno-economic analysis, feasibility studies, and policy analysis** to identify hydrogen projects that are technically feasible, financially attractive, and environmentally sustainable.

## Hydrogen Infrastructure & Ecosystem Development

Supporting the development of the wider hydrogen ecosystem, including **production, storage, transportation, refuelling, safety, standards, supply chains, workforce development, and investment frameworks**.

## Sustainable Energy Security

Our ultimate focus is to use Sri Lanka's abundant renewable energy potential to develop a **secure, sustainable, and competitive clean-energy system**, reducing dependence on imported fossil fuels while creating new opportunities for investment, innovation, employment, and economic growth.

# Our Approach

**Renewable Energy → Green Hydrogen → Industrial & Mobility Applications → Power-to-X → Investment → Commercialization**

Through this integrated approach, **H2 Lanka aims to transform green hydrogen from an emerging technology into practical projects that create measurable environmental, economic, and social value.**
