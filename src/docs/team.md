## Dr. Dayan Karunarathna – Technical Adviser 

Dr. Dayan Karunarathna is a renewable energy specialist with over 17 years of professional experience in resource assessment, clean energy planning, and sustainable energy policy development in Sri Lanka. where he contributes to national renewable energy initiatives, including resource mapping, energy forecasting, and the development of Sri Lanka's renewable hydrogen strategy.

He earned his Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Energy and Environment from Korea University, Republic of Korea. His doctoral research focused on the techno-economic analysis of green hydrogen production and its application in Sri Lanka's transportation sector, utilizing advanced energy modeling tools such as HOMER Pro, LEAP, and the HySupply Cost Tool. His research provides practical pathways for integrating renewable hydrogen into Sri Lanka's future low-carbon economy.

## Mr. Nihal Dedigama – Director / CEO

Nihal Dedigama is an international development professional with over 25 years of extensive experience in managing and delivering strategic initiatives funded by bilateral and multilateral development partners. His expertise spans sustainable development, institutional strengthening, policy advisory, programme management, and multi-stakeholder engagement across diverse sectors.  
He currently serves as a director/consultant of a leading consultancy firm as well as a policy and strategic committee member of the Sri Lanka Family Planning Association. As a consultant, he provides technical and strategic advisory support to numerous consultancy assignments supported by international development agencies regionally.  
He holds a Master’s Degree specializing in HRM, a PGDip in HRM, and a BBM, complemented by professional certifications in SME and several international training programmes in the field of international development and media public relations.

## Mr. Manoj Wanigasekara – Director 

Manoj Wanigasekara is a seasoned banking and finance professional with over 28 years of distinguished experience in the Sri Lankan banking industry, including more than a decade in senior management positions at Seylan Bank PLC. Throughout his career, he has demonstrated exceptional leadership in corporate banking, credit management, financial analysis, branch operations, regulatory compliance, risk management, and customer relationship management.

His extensive expertise in financial assessment, credit evaluation, investment analysis, and strategic decision-making has enabled him to successfully manage complex financial portfolios while ensuring sound governance, regulatory compliance, and sustainable business growth. He is recognized for his analytical capabilities, strategic thinking, and ability to build strong relationships with clients, financial institutions, investors, and key stakeholders.

As the global transition towards clean energy accelerates, Mr. Wanigasekara brings valuable financial and commercial expertise to the renewable energy and green hydrogen sectors. He actively supports the development of bankable green hydrogen and renewable energy projects by providing strategic guidance on investment evaluation, project financing, financial structuring, credit risk assessment, and stakeholder engagement. His expertise contributes to improving the financial viability of large-scale clean energy investments and facilitating access to sustainable financing mechanisms.

## Mr. Danushka Sandakalum
